Ask most creators how to grow and they'll talk about acquisition — more posts, more collabs, more reach. Ask them how they keep the people they already have, and the answer gets vague.

That's the problem. Acquisition without retention is a bucket with a hole in it. You can pour more in, but you're paying to replace what's leaking out.

## What a retention loop actually is

A retention loop is a repeatable reason for a member to come back that doesn't require you to manually create it each time. The best ones run on the community itself:

1. A member arrives and is welcomed — by a person or a well-designed automation
2. They're given something to do in the first week, not just something to watch
3. Their activity is seen and acknowledged, which pulls them back
4. That return creates activity that welcomes the *next* member

Notice that you appear in step one and then step out. The loop keeps turning whether or not you posted today.

## Signs your loop is broken

- Engagement spikes when you post and flatlines when you don't
- New members go quiet within days of joining
- The same handful of people carry every conversation

None of these are content problems. They're structural, and content won't fix them.

## Fixing it is design work

Retention is designed, not willed into being. It comes from onboarding that gives people a role, from rituals the community expects, and from automation that catches people before they drift.
